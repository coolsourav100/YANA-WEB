Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 30, 2026

Introduction

YANA ("Your AI Navigator Assistant") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards your information.

Information We Collect

Data Stored Locally

  • Travel Preferences: Your saved destinations and filters are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage
  • App Settings: Theme preferences and app configuration settings
  • Session Data: Temporary session identifiers for API communication

Data Transmitted to Servers

  • API Requests: When you search for destinations or view travel information, requests are sent to our backend servers
  • Usage Analytics: We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve app performance (no personal identifiable information)

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide personalized travel recommendations
  • To improve app functionality and user experience
  • To maintain and optimize our services
  • To communicate with you about app updates (if you opt-in)

Data Storage and Security

  • All personal data is stored locally on your device
  • We use industry-standard encryption for data transmission (HTTPS)
  • We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties

Third-Party Services

YANA may use third-party services for:

  • Analytics
  • Crash reporting
  • API services for travel information

These services have their own privacy policies, and we recommend reviewing them.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data stored in the app
  • Delete your local data by uninstalling the app
  • Opt-out of analytics (if provided in app settings)

Children's Privacy

YANA does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age.

Data Deletion Request

If you wish to request the deletion of your personal data, you can do so by:

  • Navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account within the app
  • Contacting us directly via email at support@yana-travel.com

We will process your request and delete all associated data within 30 days, except for data we are required to retain by law.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at:

Email: support@yana-travel.com
